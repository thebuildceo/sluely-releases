## 🌟 What is Sluely?

**Sluely** is an autonomous desktop AI assistant engineered for deep contextual awareness. Unlike standard chat windows, Sluely operates seamlessly alongside your active workflow — reading your screen, listening to meetings and conversations in real-time, and providing instant, intelligent suggestions and code solutions without interrupting your focus.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛡️ 1. Undetectable & Screen-Share Stealth
- **Hidden Window Technology**: Designed to stay invisible during screen sharing on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Discord.
- **Floating Overlay Mode**: Minimal, distraction-free HUD that floats above your active IDE, browser, or terminal.

### 👁️ 2. Real-Time Vision & Screen Intelligence
- **Active Window Context**: Instantly analyzes code errors, UI layouts, documentation, or technical questions on your screen.
- **One-Click Screen Analysis**: Capture any region or active window and ask for explanations, fixes, or refactoring in seconds.

### 🎙️ 3. Live Audio Transcription & Meeting Copilot
- **Dual-Stream Audio Engine**: Transcribes both system output (interviewers, teammates, webinars) and your microphone simultaneously.
- **Real-Time Humanizer & Answers**: Delivers live context-aware talking points, code snippets, and answers during live sessions.

### 🔑 4. 100% BYOK — Zero Subscriptions, Total Control
- No monthly paywalls or hidden credits.
- Plug in your own API keys directly:
  - **Anthropic Claude** (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Haiku, Opus)
  - **OpenAI** (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, o1)
  - **Google Gemini** (Gemini 1.5 Pro & Flash, 2.0 Flash)
  - **DeepSeek** (DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1)
  - **Groq & Local LLMs** (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM)

### 🔒 5. Privacy & Security First
- **Local Storage**: Your API keys and chat history are encrypted and stored locally on your machine.
- **Zero Telemetry Leaks**: Queries communicate strictly between your device and your chosen AI provider's official API.

## 📥 Installation Guide

### Windows (x64)

1. **Download the latest installer**:
   - [👉 Click here to Download Sluely-Setup-3.0.0.exe](https://api.v2.sluely.com/desktop-download/win/latest.exe)
2. **Run the Installer**:
   - Double-click `Sluely-Setup-3.0.0.exe`.
   - *Note for Windows SmartScreen:* If Windows displays a "Windows protected your PC" prompt, click **"More info"** ➔ **"Run anyway"** (this occurs because the installer is freshly released and not yet signed with an enterprise EV certificate).
3. **Launch Sluely**:
   - Open Sluely from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.
   - Go to **Settings ➔ AI Providers** and paste your API key (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek).
   - You're all set!
